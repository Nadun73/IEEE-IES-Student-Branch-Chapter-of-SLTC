# Focus Areas image prompts

All four source images were created with the built-in image generation tool.
The website uses 900 × 900 JPEG derivatives; the 1254 × 1254 PNG files are
retained as source assets.

## Automation & Control

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: website focus-area section illustration
Primary request: Automation & Control — a clean modern industrial robotic arm operating over a compact automated conveyor cell, with a small sensor/control module that clearly suggests sensing and closed-loop control.
Scene/backdrop: pure white seamless background with no horizon line or environment.
Subject: one compact industrial robotic arm beside a short automated conveyor; a small optical sensor and compact control module monitor a simple workpiece, with neat visible feedback wiring connecting sensor, controller, and robot base and a subtle restrained sensing beam or scan cue. The machinery should feel plausible, efficient, and uncluttered.
Style/medium: polished minimal 3D isometric technical illustration; refined product-render quality; simplified geometric forms; crisp clean object edges.
Composition/framing: centered three-quarter isometric composition, fully contained in frame, balanced footprint, generous whitespace on every side, no cropping.
Lighting/mood: bright neutral studio lighting, soft subtle contact shadows only, clean and optimistic technical mood.
Color palette: dominated by navy #142D52 and IEEE blue #006495, with restrained orange #E47726 accents only; white and very light neutral grays for secondary surfaces.
Materials/textures: smooth powder-coated metal, matte polymer housings, subtle brushed-metal conveyor details; clean and lightly reflective without photorealistic clutter.
Constraints: sensing and closed-loop control must be visually understandable through the sensor, control module, feedback connection, and robot action; pure white background; centered minimal composition; no people; no text; no labels; no numbers; no logos; no branding; no watermark; no border; no decorative UI overlays.
Avoid: busy factory background, extra machinery, floating arrows, callout lines, text-like markings, excessive orange, dark background, dramatic cinematic lighting, heavy shadows, grunge, photorealistic warehouse scene, cropped objects.
```
